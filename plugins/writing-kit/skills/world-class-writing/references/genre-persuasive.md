# Genre Profile: Persuasive, Product & Copywriting (`persuasive`)

**Target Documents**: Product Launch Pages, Landing Copy, B2B/B2C Value Propositions, Customer Proposals, Pitch Narratives, Feature Announcements.
**Starred Dimensions (must be `>= 2` each)**: **W1 (Audience Psychology & JTBD) ★, W5 (Concrete Proof & Signal Density) ★, W7 (Authentic Voice, Zero Hype) ★**.

---

## 1. Structural Architecture

World-class persuasion wins by **clarity and verifiable proof**, never by loud adjectives or manipulative urgency:

1. **Sharp Positioning & Specific Reader Mirror (Above the Fold)**:
   - Name the exact user, their concrete job-to-be-done (JTBD), and the tangible outcome in plain language.
   - Pass the "Competitor Swap Test": if a competitor could paste their logo onto your headline and it still reads identically, rewrite it.
2. **Cost of Status Quo (Recognizable Pain, Not Fear-Mongering)**:
   - Describe the exact workflow friction, wasted time, or error mode the reader already lives through — with concrete specificity so they think *"this describes my Tuesday."*
3. **Mechanism + Verifiable Proof**:
   - Explain *how* the product or proposal removes the friction in concrete mechanics, not magic.
   - Pair every benefit claim with proof (before/after workflow, verified customer metric, live constraint solved, or explicit `[확인 필요: 검증 지표]` if not yet measured).
4. **Objection & Risk Reversal (Honest Boundaries)**:
   - Address the buyer's #1 skepticism directly (migration effort, lock-in, learning curve, who this is *not* for). Admitting real boundaries dramatically raises credibility.
5. **Single Clear Next Action (CTA)**:
   - End with one low-friction, concrete call to action stating exactly what happens next (never three competing CTAs).

---

## 2. Genre-Specific Editorial Rules

1. **Ban Hype Adjectives — Show Mechanism Instead**:
   - Cut `혁신적인`, `압도적인`, `최고의`, `완벽한`, `게임 체인저`, `revolutionary`, `seamless`, `world-class`, `next-generation`.
   - Replace with observable delta (`"혁신적인 배포 속도"` → `"수동 설정 없이 30초 만에 3개 CLI 검증 완료"`).
2. **You-Orientation Over We-Bragging**:
   - Flip company-centric sentences (`"우리는 최신 아키텍처를 제공합니다"`) into reader-outcome sentences (`"설정을 한 번만 고치면 세 도구의 어댑터가 자동으로 동기화됩니다"`).
3. **Micro-Scannability & Crisp Cadence**:
   - Keep paragraphs short (2–4 sentences), headlines assertion-driven, and verbs concrete.
   - Maintain ethical honesty: zero fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, or unverified ROI claims (`P0` violation).

---

## 3. Audit Calibration (`--genre persuasive`)

- **Strict checks**: Zero fabricated ROI/social-proof numbers, zero buzzword/hype AI slop (`혁신적인 솔루션`, `패러다임 전환`, `game-changer`, `seamlessly`), high concrete-anchor density, tight sentence lengths (short punchy cadence), clean Korean active voice.
- **Thesis Gate**: Passes both **Competitor Swap Test** and **Negation Test** (clear differentiation with explicit trade-off or scope focus).
