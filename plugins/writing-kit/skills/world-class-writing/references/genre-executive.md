# Genre Profile: Executive & Strategic Writing (`executive`)

**Target Documents**: Amazon 6-Pager Narrative Memos, Decision Memos, Strategic Proposals, Board/Leadership Updates, Policy & Investment Briefs.
**Starred Dimensions (must be `>= 2` each)**: **W1 (Audience & Decision Intent) ★, W2 (Epistemic Rigor & Counter-Argument) ★, W3 (Macro-Structure BLUF/SCQA) ★**.

---

## 1. Structural Architecture

### A. BLUF + Minto Pyramid + SCQA Spine
Executives read top-down under severe time pressure. Never write a mystery novel that hides the answer at the bottom:
1. **Decision Ask / Executive Answer (First Paragraph — BLUF)**: State the recommendation, exact resource/scope ask, and decision deadline in the opening 3–5 sentences.
2. **SCQA Framing (1 Short Section)**:
   - **Situation**: Undisputed current baseline (1–2 sentences max — do not lecture executives on what they already know).
   - **Complication**: The concrete shift, bottleneck, or risk that makes status quo untenable.
   - **Question**: The decision fork now on the table.
   - **Answer**: Your single clear recommendation.
3. **MECE Supporting Pillars (2–4 Assertion Sections)**:
   - Every heading MUST be a **complete assertion** (`"평가 자동화를 선행하면 분기 배포 결함을 40% 줄일 수 있다"`), never a vague label (`"현황 분석"`, `"기대 효과"`).
   - Each pillar carries verifiable evidence, unit economics or operational mechanism, and explicit scope boundaries.
4. **Steelmanned Risks, Trade-Offs & Rejected Alternatives**:
   - Present the strongest objection to your own proposal fairly (Amazon FAQ / Bar-Raiser standard).
   - Explain why Option B / "Do Nothing" loses on the deciding criterion, and specify pre-mortem triggers ("What signal would prove us wrong within 60 days?").
5. **Execution Plan & Single-Threaded Ownership**: Milestones, DRI (Directly Responsible Individual), measurable exit metrics, and explicit approval ask.

### B. Amazon 6-Pager Narrative Rule (When Requested)
- Write in **connected prose paragraphs**, not slide-deck bullet fragments — complete sentences expose hidden logical leaps that bullets disguise.
- Use tables strictly for comparative matrices, timelines, or financial/metric breakdowns.

---

## 2. Genre-Specific Editorial Rules

1. **Zero Hidden Assumptions**: Separate **Observed Fact** from **Inference** and **Recommendation**. Mark unverified metrics `[확인 필요: ...]` — guessing revenue, conversion, or cost numbers is an immediate `P0` fail.
2. **Quantify Trade-Offs (Opportunity Cost)**: Every "yes" implies a "no." State explicitly what gets delayed, cut, or risked to fund this recommendation.
3. **No Corporate Fog**: Cut `시너지 극대화`, `패러다임 전환`, `전사적 역량 결집`, `leverage synergies`, `strategic alignment`. Name the exact team, workflow, metric, and date.
4. **Register**: Calm, objective, decision-ready authority. Avoid exclamation marks, hype adjectives, and defensive hedging.

---

## 3. Audit Calibration (`--genre executive`)

- **Strict checks**: Zero fabricated numbers, low hedge/weasel density, zero vague label headings (`배경`, `개요`, `결론`), strong concrete-anchor density (dates, owners, costs, metrics), clean Korean active prose free of translationese and double passives.
- **Thesis Gate**: Passes **Negation Test** — an executive could reasonably reject the recommendation in favor of another allocation of capital or engineering time.
